Advanced Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

1--Customer Health Score

Automatically calculate a customer health score using configurable signals.

For example:

Unresolved support tickets ---	Customer risk
Subscription expiry approaching  ---	Renewal risk

Display customer statuses such as:
- Healthy
- Needs Attention
- At Risk
- Inactive


2--Customer Segmentation

Create dynamic customer groups:
- Free customers.
- Paid customers.
- Enterprise customers.
- High-revenue customers.
- Customers with multiple applications.
- Customers with no activity in 30 days.
- Customers with failed builds.
- Customers with upcoming renewals.



Advanced Application Monitoring & Management

1--App Version Management

Maintain a version history for every application.
Information  --	Example
Current Version  --	1.0.5
Previous Version  --	1.0.4
Platform  --	Android
Release Status  --	Published

Additional actions:
- View version history.
- Download previous builds.
- Compare configuration changes.
- Roll back to a previous configuration.


2--Application Configuration Override

Super Admin should be able to modify specific application settings:
- App name.
- App icon.
- Splash screen.
- Primary theme.
- Navigation layout.
- Color palette.
- Typography.
- Feature access.

3--Application Status Controls

Provide administrative controls:
- Active.
- Suspended.
- Under Maintenance.
- Build Restricted.
- Temporarily Disabled.
When suspending an application, require a reason and provide an option to notify the customer.



Advanced Subscription & Revenue Intelligence

1--Revenue Analytics

Track:
- Gross revenue.
- Net revenue.
- Monthly recurring revenue.
- Annual recurring revenue.

2--Failed Payment Recovery

If a customer's payment fails:
1. Record the failed transaction.
2. Notify the customer.
3. Allow payment retry.
4. Track retry attempts.
5. Update subscription status based on payment confirmation.


3--Advanced Discount Management

Create promotional offers:
- Percentage discounts.
- Fixed amount discounts.
- Coupon codes.
- First-purchase discounts.
- Renewal discounts.
- Customer-specific discounts.
- Limited-time offers.

4--Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

Calculate the revenue generated by each customer throughout their relationship with the platform.
